Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by B.J. Baizly TECH CO., LTD. (doing business as Baizly), a second-hand air-conditioning compressor recycling and supply company based in Beijing, China. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at haow@wqsxb.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect information that you voluntarily submit through our contact form and direct messages:

  • Name — to address you and identify your inquiry.
  • Company — to understand your business and serving market.
  • Country — to estimate shipping and confirm we can serve your region.
  • Email and contact number — to reply to your inquiry.
  • Target model / quantity / notes — to prepare a quotation and check availability.

We do not collect payment-card data on this site. Payments are settled by bank wire or through your own payment provider; we never receive or store your card details.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and prepare quotations.
  • To confirm stock, pricing, lead time and the shipping plan before any order.
  • To arrange inspection, packing, export customs clearance and delivery.
  • To communicate with you by email, WhatsApp or WeChat — whichever you choose.

4. Legal basis (GDPR & CCPA)

For visitors in the European Economic Area, we process your data on the basis of consent (you submit the form) and our legitimate interest in responding to a business inquiry. For California residents, this notice is provided under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information, and we have never sold it. We share data only to the minimum extent needed to serve you:

  • With freight forwarders and carriers, solely to fulfil an agreed shipment.
  • With the messaging platform you choose (e.g. WhatsApp, WeChat, email provider) to communicate with you.
  • Where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

6. Data hosting and international transfers

This website is hosted on a web server located in the United States. Inquiry messages are delivered to our team via WeCom (Enterprise WeChat), a business messaging service. By submitting the form, you acknowledge that your information may be processed in these jurisdictions.

7. Data retention

We keep inquiry records only as long as necessary for our business relationship and to meet legal or trade-record obligations (for example, records related to export shipments), after which they are deleted or anonymised.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may object to its processing. California residents may request to know what data we hold and ask us to delete it. To exercise any of these rights, email haow@wqsxb.com.

9. Cookies and analytics

We do not run advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. This site is served through a content-delivery network (Cloudflare) that may set minimal technical cookies strictly necessary for security and caching. No personal profile is built from your browsing.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be posted on this page.
