Overview

The ZP24K3E-PFJ-522 is a 2 HP R410A scroll compressor designed for ducted splits, VRF modules and compact chiller packages. Compared with rotary units of similar capacity it runs cooler under part load, which is why contractors specify it for systems that cycle often.

  • Available with corresponding replacement options to control cost
