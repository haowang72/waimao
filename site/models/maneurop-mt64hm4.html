Overview

MT64HM4 belongs to the Maneurop MT scroll family used across cold rooms and medium-temperature condensing units. It is a go-to choice in refrigeration retrofit work where an older semi-hermetic unit is being replaced with a quieter, more efficient scroll.

Key points

  • Proven MT platform with wide service familiarity
  • Suitable for both air conditioning and medium-temperature refrigeration
  • Retrofit friendly: fit-for-fit with many legacy cabinets
  • Spares and service parts readily available in most markets

Applications

  • Cold room condensing units
  • Medium-temperature refrigeration racks
  • Packaged chillers and air handlers

Compatibility & replacement notes

Frequently used in R22 systems transitioning to R407C; POE oil is normally required for HFC blends when retrofitting.
