Overview

FFI12HBK is a classic 1/2 HP high back pressure hermetic compressor for R134a. It is used in beverage coolers, display cases and ice machines where the evaporating temperature stays relatively high and reliability over long running hours matters more than peak capacity.

Key points

  • High-efficiency design reduces cabinet running cost
  • HBP class — built for chillers and beverage cabinets that run warm-side
  • Quiet operation suitable for front-of-house equipment
  • Available in both original and qualified replacement grades

Applications

  • Beverage coolers and display cabinets
  • Ice makers and water dispensers
  • Household refrigerators and freezers
