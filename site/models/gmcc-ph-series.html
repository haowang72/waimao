Overview

The GMCC PH series covers the most heavily traded rotary compressors in the 0.75–3 HP band. For distributors, this family matters because it maps directly onto the residential split market: whatever capacity your local brands sell, there is usually a PH-series equivalent behind it.

Key points

  • One platform, multiple capacities — simplifies spares planning
  • R410A and R32 versions for current-efficiency markets
  • Fixed-speed and inverter options available
  • Stable monthly output for contract buyers
