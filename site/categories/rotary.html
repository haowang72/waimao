Rotary Air Conditioner Compressors — Second-Hand Wholesale Supply

Single-phase rotary compressors for split AC, window units and portable air conditioners from 0.5 HP to 3 HP, R22 / R410A / R32 designs.

Rotary compressors are the workhorse of residential and light commercial air conditioning. They run quietly, cost less than scroll equivalents in small capacities, and are easier to service in the field.

We supply fixed-speed and inverter rotary compressors from 0.5 HP to 3 HP, covering R22, R410A and R32 refrigerants with 220–240 V / 50 Hz and 208–230 V / 60 Hz windings for different export markets.

Buying checklist

  • Match refrigerant exactly — an R410A compressor cannot replace an R22 unit without system redesign.
  • We supply 220–240 V / 50 Hz and 208–230 V / 60 Hz windings — tell us your market and we will match.
  • Ask whether the unit ships with the accumulator, mounting studs and start accessories.
  • For tropical markets we can supply T3-rated units — let us know your ambient class.
