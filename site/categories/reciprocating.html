Hermetic Reciprocating Compressors — Second-Hand Wholesale Supply

RSIR / CSIR / RSCR reciprocating compressors for freezers, refrigerators, cold rooms, ice machines, display cases and beverage coolers.

Hermetic reciprocating compressors remain the standard for light commercial refrigeration. Compact, reliable and easy to match to cabinets, they cover everything from domestic freezers to cold rooms and ice machines.

We stock LBP (low back pressure), MBP (medium back pressure) and HBP (high back pressure) series in R134a and R600a versions, with cabinet-style models for beverages and display cases.

Every unit is packed for export with palletized cartons, and we can arrange third-party inspection before shipment.
